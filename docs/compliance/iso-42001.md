# ISO/IEC 42001

Not legal advice, and not a statement that Tracekit meets any clause or control ([about these pages](README.md)).

## Scope

ISO/IEC 42001:2023, *Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Management system*, was published in December
2023 (edition 1; no amendment had been published on the date we checked). It sets requirements for an AI management
system in an organisation that provides or uses AI systems. It is voluntary, and organisations can be certified against
it. Clauses 4 to 10 are requirements on the management system; Annex A (normative) lists reference control objectives
and controls, and Annex B gives implementation guidance for them. ISO's text is copyrighted, so this page cites clause
and control numbers and titles only: read the standard for what each one requires. The Annex A titles come from public
sources that agree with each other (see Sources), not from the standard itself. A management system is people,
processes and records; a tool is at most one of its controls. Each row says what Tracekit can contribute to a clause or
control, and what the clause still needs from you.

## Not covered by Tracekit

- **Clauses 4 and 5:** context of the organisation, leadership, AI policy, roles at management level.
- **6.1.2, 6.1.4, 8.2 and 8.4:** AI risk assessment and AI system impact assessment. Tracekit's records can inform
  them; they are yours to do.
- **6.2, 6.3 and 7.1 to 7.4:** objectives, planning of changes, resources, competence, awareness and communication.
- **9.3 Management review** and **10.1 Continual improvement.**
- **Annex A families A.2 (policies related to AI), A.4 (resources for AI systems), A.5 (assessing impacts of AI
  systems) and A.7 (data for AI systems).**
- **A.6.1 and A.6.2.2 to A.6.2.5:** responsible development, requirements, design documentation, verification and
  validation, deployment. **A.6.2.7 AI system technical documentation** is yours to write; it can cite Tracekit's
  public docs.
- **A.3.3 Reporting of concerns, A.8.2, A.8.3 and A.8.5:** information for users, external reporting and information
  for interested parties. **A.9.3 Objectives for responsible use.**
