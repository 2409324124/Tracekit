Verify a Tracekit bundle

The TypeScript verifier of Tracekit , in this page. Choose a .tkb bundle and your trust config: the log key and witnesses you pin, never ones the bundle brings. The page reads both files here; its Content Security Policy lets it load and send nothing, so they never leave your browser.



The report and its verdicts are those of npx @cygnuxlabs/tracekit verify. A bundle that relies on a
check this verifier doesn't implement (SLH-DSA checkpoint signatures, Rekor anchors, record-key certificates, tracekit
why findings) is UNVERIFIABLE, never VERIFIED: verify it with tracekit verify. What each line means:
docs/verdicts.md in the Tracekit repository.
